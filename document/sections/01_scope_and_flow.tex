\section{System Scope and End-to-End Flow}
\label{sec:scope}

SPIRA uses a shared purchase order and bounded household context to infer
purchase intent, generate relevant preparation tasks, and connect those tasks
to observable household results. Synthetic purchase-intent cases provide
retrieval evidence for runtime inference. Execution and user feedback then
contribute to household memory. \sourcecite{draft}

\subsection{Episode flow}

\begin{center}
\small
Shared order $\rightarrow$ item extraction and structuring\\[2pt]
$\rightarrow$ bounded context assembly and knowledge retrieval\\[2pt]
$\rightarrow$ purchase-intent inference $\rightarrow$ task generation\\[2pt]
$\rightarrow$ versioned plan and notification $\rightarrow$ Home execution\\[2pt]
$\rightarrow$ device/simulator observation and feedback\\[2pt]
$\rightarrow$ household memory
\end{center}

An order can support a household situation, ordinary replenishment, or no
special intent. Empty preparation is a valid outcome. In a mixed order,
unrelated items remain outside the inferred situation. The current baseline
supports device-state adjustment, bounded delivered-item care, environmental
preparation, and selected loss-prevention notices or refrigerator-screen
guidance. Autonomous preparation is bounded by capability and product policy.
\sourcecite{baseline}

\subsection{Participants and authority}

{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.23\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Participant} & \textbf{Responsibility and authority} \\
\midrule
Knowledge Base & Maintains synthetic and permitted actual purchase-intent
cases, labels, embeddings, and the retrieval population. \\
Service & Processes orders, assembles inference context, retrieves evidence,
infers intent, generates tasks, and finalizes plans and app-facing results.
It decides the intended preparation and its rationale. \\
App & Shares orders and displays the current plan, rationale, results, and
user stop or line-off controls. \\
Home & Checks current household/device truth and execution eligibility,
evaluates local triggers, dispatches commands, obtains readback, and handles
user override and restoration. \\
Simulator / Device Connector & Exposes connector-visible state, raw events,
command application, and observable results with their provenance. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\par
}

Service uses Home results to interpret what actually occurred. The Knowledge
Base supplies inference evidence, while device command authority remains at
the execution boundary. \sourcecite{draft}

\subsection{Birthday-party reference episode}

The current scenario combines a Saturday birthday calendar entry with a mixed
order containing princess-party supplies, cupcake ingredients, strawberries,
and routine items. Relevant preparation can include cleaning, supported child
safety settings, party atmosphere, oven standby, and strawberry guidance.
The App presents one preparation card with stop and per-task line-off controls.
Device integration assumptions remain subject to available connectors or
explicitly identified simulation. \sourcecite{baseline}
